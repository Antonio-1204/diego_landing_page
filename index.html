FAQ

Everything you want to know

How far in advance should we book?

Ideally 9 to 12 months ahead, especially for Saturdays in spring and fall. If your wedding is sooner, reach out anyway: we sometimes have open dates.

How do we secure our date?

With a signed contract and a 30% retainer. The balance is due 15 days before the wedding, by bank transfer or card.

Do you travel for weddings?

We love to! We've worked across Florida, New York, California and Mexico. Travel costs (flights, lodging and meals) are quoted separately.

When will we receive our photos and film?

A 30-photo sneak peek within 72 hours, the full gallery in about 6 weeks and your film in about 10 weeks. The social media teaser arrives in 7 days.

Can we choose the music for our film?

Yes. Tell us the style you love and we'll pick licensed tracks together, so you can post your film on social media without it getting muted.

What if it rains or our date changes?

We always have a plan B for light and portraits. If you need to move your date, we transfer your retainer to a new day, subject to availability.
